# 当劳动不再足以证明人的价值：智能涌现、社会承认与代际未来的意义危机

When Work No Longer Suffices to Establish Human Worth: Emergent Intelligence, Social Recognition, and a Crisis of Meaning in Intergenerational Futures

如果技术减少了社会对某些劳动的需要，人却仍须通过劳动证明自己的价值，会发生什么？本文讨论智能能力变化如何通过收入、实践、承认与未来预期影响人的生活，并审视它与代际关切的可能联系。

## 论文简介

文章以社会理论与规范论证为主，结合问题导向的文献分析和 Lee 等公开资料的既有二次分析。公开保留实验记录为 269 人，另有 270 人调查；本文没有新增访谈、问卷或参与者。

- 生产能力提高不会自动分配收益、赋予实际参与或提供可支配时间；人的基本地位应与生产绩效区分。
- 公开实验中，直接复制 AI 组的首次任务意义均值比独立完成组低 0.597 分；调查中的一般工作意义相关接近零，限制了笼统的负面叙述。
- 从工作变化通向意义体验、再通向未来和生育意愿的路径是有条件的假说；文章同时讨论反例、生育自主与不能被轻易补偿的转型损失。

未证明 AI 导致低生育或人类整体失去意义。实验公开记录是 408 名完成者中的筛选子集，不能恢复其余 139 人；短期任务与横断面调查不识别完整长期机制。思想实验不是预测。

两份 PDF 均含完整正文、方法、限制与参考文献。计算复核与 AI 辅助自查的范围在正文说明，不等同于外部学术审稿。

## English

### About the paper

What happens when society needs less of certain labor but people are still expected to establish their worth through work? The paper examines income, practice, recognition, and expectations of the future, including possible connections to intergenerational concerns.

**[Complete English PDF](en.pdf) · [Chinese PDF](zh.pdf) · [Discussion and feedback](https://github.com/lawyer112/20261026-ai-work-meaning/issues)**

Author: lawyer112 · Paper ID: 20261026-ai-work-meaning · Content version: 0.4.2 · Edition date: 28 October 2026

Social theory and normative argument are informed by a problem-oriented literature analysis and an existing secondary analysis of Lee and colleagues’ public records: 269 retained experimental cases and a separate survey of 270 people. No new interviews, surveys, or participants were added.

- Greater productive capacity does not automatically distribute gains, confer substantive participation, or provide freely usable time. Basic standing should be distinguished from productive performance.
- In the public experiment, initial task meaning was 0.597 points lower for the copying group than for independent work. General-work meaning correlations in the survey were near zero, constraining broad negative claims.
- Paths from changing work to meaning and then to future expectations and fertility intentions are conditional hypotheses. Counterexamples, reproductive autonomy, and potentially nonfungible transition losses remain central.

The paper does not establish that AI causes low fertility or a general loss of meaning. The public experiment is a selected subset of 408 completers, and the other 139 cannot be recovered. Short-term tasks and a cross-sectional survey do not identify the full long-term mechanism. Thought experiments are not forecasts.

Both PDFs contain the complete text, methods, limitations, and references. The paper explains the scope of computational verification and AI-assisted self-checks; these are not external academic review.
